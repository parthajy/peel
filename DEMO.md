# Recording the demo

Turn on **Demo mode** in the toolbar popup (gear → Demo mode). Peel then draws an orange cursor halo, a ripple on every click, a badge for every keypress and a caption naming each flip, all inside the page, so any screen recorder captures them.

Use the **in-page picker** (tap the corner), not the toolbar popup. Recorders that capture a window or tab do not see the popup.

Keep it under 60 seconds. Suggested script, one site per beat:

1. **Hacker News, Original.** Hover the corner so it lifts. Drag it slowly up-left: Paper curls in. Let the newspaper breathe for two seconds.
2. **Shift+F three times** on the same page: Dark, Noir, Glowy. The key badge shows each press.
3. **Tap the corner**, scroll the picker past Eras and Systems, pick **macOS**. Pick **Typewriter**. Pick **1996** and click once so the modem plays (turn sounds on if you record audio).
4. **A news site** (BBC or NDTV): Paper again, then open the picker, choose Flip → **Shatter**, pick Snow. Scroll for five seconds so snow piles on the headlines.
5. **Toys.** Pick **Blast**, click twice. Pick **Hammer**, hit one card three times. Pick **Tear**, drag from the left edge across. Press **R**: everything rewinds.
6. **LinkedIn in Pest Infected.** Squish three bugs. Then Shift+P back to Original and let the cat walk by in Light.

End on the picker open, showing the grid of swatches.

Record at 1920×1080 or 2560×1440, 30 or 60 fps. Hide bookmarks bar and other tabs. If using macOS screen recording, enable "Show mouse clicks" too, but Demo mode makes that optional.
