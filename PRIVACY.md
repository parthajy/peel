# Peel privacy policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

Peel is a browser extension that restyles the web pages you visit. It runs entirely on your device.

**What Peel collects:** nothing. Peel has no servers, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, accounts, advertising or third-party code.

**What Peel stores on your device:** your chosen edition, remixes and settings (in Chrome's synced extension storage, so they follow your Chrome profile), and a few local-only items: sites where you turned Peel off, per-site visit counts if you enable Patina, bug-squish scores, and spray-paint walls you chose to keep. You can clear all of it by removing the extension.

**Screenshots:** to animate a flip between looks, Peel takes a screenshot of the current tab using Chrome's built-in API. The image is held in memory for under a second to drive the animation and is then discarded. It is never written to disk or transmitted. The "Save a shot" button creates a PNG only when you press it, and saves it to your Downloads folder.

**Page content:** Peel reads the structure of the page in order to restyle it. Nothing it reads leaves the page.
